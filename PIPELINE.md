# 🎯 Client Hunting Pipeline — LumoraCode

## Overview

**Goal:** Find non-technical business owners in US/UK/AUS/CAN who need a website, then close them via cold call, email, or DM.

**Weapons:**
- 🇬🇧 UK phone number (cold calls)
- ✉️ Cold emails (personalized, not templates)
- 💬 DMs (LinkedIn, Instagram, Facebook)
- 🤖 Lux (lead research + script/email generation)

---

## Phase 1: FIND — Lead Sourcing

### Target Profile
- **WHO:** Small business owners, solo practitioners, local service providers
- **WHERE:** US, UK, Canada, Australia, UAE (high purchasing power)
- **INDUSTRIES (priority):**
  1. 🏥 Private doctors/dentists/clinics (no website or terrible website)
  2. ⚖️ Solo lawyers / small law firms
  3. 🏠 Real estate agents / property managers
  4. 💅 Salon/spa/beauty business owners
  5. 🔧 Plumbing/HVAC/electrical contractors
  6. 🍕 Restaurants / cafés (no online presence)
  7. 📸 Photographers / videographers
  8. 🏋️ Personal trainers / gym owners
  9. 🧹 Cleaning / home service companies
  10. 📊 Consultants / coaches

### Sourcing Channels

#### Social Media (Daily mining)
| Platform | What to Search | Signal |
|---|---|---|
| **Instagram** | Local business hashtags (#dentistlondon, #nycplumber), bio says "DM for booking" but no website link | No website in bio, or links to just a Facebook page |
| **Facebook** | Business pages with no website, local groups where owners ask for help | "Does anyone know a web developer?" posts |
| **LinkedIn** | Business owners with no company website, posts about "needing an online presence" | Profile says CEO/Founder but company has no URL |
| **TikTok** | Small biz owners posting content but no website in bio | Growing audience, no way to convert |
| **Google Maps** | Search "[industry] near [city]" — filter for businesses with no website | Google listing exists but "Website" button is missing |
| **Yelp** | Businesses with good reviews but no website linked | Relying on Yelp as their only online presence |
| **Reddit** | r/smallbusiness, r/Entrepreneur, r/startups — people asking about websites | "Should I get a website?" "How much does a website cost?" |
| **Twitter/X** | Small biz owners tweeting about business struggles | "I really need to get a website" type posts |

#### Direct Research (Weekly deep dives)
- **Google Maps scraping:** Pick a city + industry, find businesses with no website
- **Yelp/Trustpilot:** Find well-reviewed businesses with bad/no websites
- **Chamber of Commerce directories:** Local business listings
- **Industry-specific directories:** Healthgrades (doctors), Avvo (lawyers), Houzz (contractors)

### Lead Qualification Checklist
- [ ] Business is active and operational
- [ ] No website OR website is outdated/broken/not mobile-friendly
- [ ] Located in high-income market (US/UK/CAN/AUS/UAE)
- [ ] Has online presence elsewhere (social media, reviews) — shows they care
- [ ] Can find contact info (email, phone, DM)
- [ ] Industry matches our target list

## Phase 2: RESEARCH — Pre-Call Intel

Before reaching out to ANY lead, gather:

1. **Business name & type**
2. **Owner's name** (LinkedIn, Facebook, Google)
3. **Current online presence** (social links, Google listing, existing site)
4. **Pain points** (no bookings system? no online menu? no portfolio?)
5. **Competitors** — find 1-2 competitors with great websites to reference
6. **Specific value prop** — what would a website DO for their business?

This intel feeds directly into the script/email generator.

## Phase 3: REACH — Multi-Channel Outreach

### Channel Priority (by conversion rate)
1. **📞 Cold Call** (UK number) — highest conversion, most personal
2. **✉️ Cold Email** — scalable, can personalize at volume
3. **💬 LinkedIn DM** — professional context, good for B2B
4. **📱 Instagram/Facebook DM** — casual, good for local businesses
5. **💬 WhatsApp** (if number available) — very personal, high open rate

### Outreach Cadence (per lead)
```
Day 1: Email #1 (intro + value)
Day 2: LinkedIn connect request + note
Day 3: Cold call attempt #1
Day 5: Email #2 (case study / social proof)
Day 7: Cold call attempt #2
Day 10: Email #3 (breakup email — "last try")
Day 14: Final DM on social media
```

### Script Framework (for Lux to customize per lead)

```
"Hi [Name], this is Taha from LumoraCode. I came across your
```

## Phase 4: CLOSE — Conversion

### Discovery Call Agenda (15 min)
1. Build rapport (2 min) — compliment their business, ask about their story
2. Understand needs (5 min) — what's their biggest challenge? How do clients find them now?
3. Present solution (5 min) — show relevant portfolio, explain what you'd build
4. Handle objections (2 min)
5. Next steps (1 min) — "I'll send you a proposal by [day]"

### Common Objections & Responses
| Objection | Response |
|---|---|
| "I can't afford it" | "What's your budget? I work with businesses at different levels. Even a one-page site can bring in more clients than no site." |
| "I'm too busy" | "That's exactly why you need a website — it works for you 24/7. I handle everything, you just approve the design." |
| "I already get clients from word of mouth" | "That's great! A website amplifies that. When someone Googles you after a referral, they should find a professional site, not nothing." |
| "I tried before and it didn't work" | "I hear that a lot. Most website builders give you a template. I build something custom for YOUR business and YOUR clients." |
| "Let me think about it" | "Of course. I'll send you a mockup of what your site could look like — no cost, no obligation. If you love it, we talk. If not, no hard feelings." |
